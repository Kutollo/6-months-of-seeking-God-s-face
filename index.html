Purpose of the Journey

This journey from July to December is a sacred commitment to seek God's face with intentionality. Through fasting, daily scripture, prayer, and reflection, we aim to deepen our relationship with the Lord, grow in spiritual discipline, and align our lives with His divine purpose.

July: Focused on seeking God's face, developing a habit of daily intimacy through prayer, scripture, and reflection. This month was one of connection, focus, and intentional pursuit of His presence.

August: Focused on turning back to God with humble hearts, walking in obedience, and aligning with His Word. This month was one of repentance, renewal, and redirection.

October (In progress): Focused on returning to God with our whole hearts: coming home to His presence, resting in His mercy, and drawing near to Him each day. This month is one of return, restoration, and rest.
